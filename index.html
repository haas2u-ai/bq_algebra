The BQ algebra, one matrix product at a time

In the BQ-Pauli algebra a physical four-vector is four real coordinates on a basis of 2×2 complex matrices, A = a0T̂ + a·K̂, with K̂ = (Î, Ĵ, K̂). Multiplying such vectors is ordinary matrix multiplication, and the algebra sorts every product into its own channels. These eight interactive models build that up step by step, and every result on them is computed live from the explicit matrices.

Eight interactive models

In teaching order: the basis products, then the bilinear of two four-vectors, then the triple product that carries Maxwell’s equations and the Lorentz force, then the quadratics of the fields themselves, and the Lorentz transformations under which all of them are covariant.

Lorentz transformations

Step 6 · changing frame for products

Lorentz transformation of the bilinear

Boost both factors of C = ATB for any pair of the papers’ four-vectors. The adjoint carries the opposite rotor, so the bilinear transforms by a similarity, CL = UCU−1: the norm stays, and the K̂ and σ̂ channels mix like B and E/c. Shown in seven forms, from the matrix and the rotor to Minkowski’s complex field transformation.

Step 7 · equations in every frame

Lorentz covariance of the triple product

A triple E = D(ATB) is boosted like a four-vector again, EL = U−1EU−1, and both its minquat and its pauliquat sector are four-vectors. So ∂B = μ0J, JB = F and the fluid equations keep their form, checked with boosted vectors and with fields differentiated in the boosted coordinates.

Step 8 · gravity as a local rotor

The gravity rotor: from rapidity to metric

Newtonian, Hubble and azimuthal rapidity fields feed a local Lorentz rotor G(r, t). Its action on dR is exact and its quadratic flat; the gravitational content is in the choice of a Galilean coframe with a Lorentzian quadratic, which gives the riverbed and gμν. Compared with Schwarzschild, de Sitter/FLRW, Schwarzschild–de Sitter and a rotating frame, and tested with its Einstein tensor.

Reproduce in Python

The web pages compute in JavaScript. The folder py_sources holds an independent Python implementation with SymPy. It checks every channel rule exactly along two routes: term by term with the multiplication table, as on these pages, and as an explicit 2×2 matrix product decomposed on the eight basis elements.

  • bq_pauli_table.py · the 64 basis products against the paper’s table
  • bq_bilinear.py · the channels of ATB, all 169 pairs
  • bq_triple.py · the four channels of D(ATB), all 2197 triples
  • bq_quadratics.py · FF and FTF for the EM, fluid and other fields
  • bq_lorentz.py · AL = U−1AU−1, adjoints, invariance, step 5 and the Lemma
  • bq_bilinear_lorentz.py · CL = UCU−1 for all 169 pairs, basis rules and complex form
  • bq_triple_lorentz.py · EL = U−1EU−1 for all 2197 triples, both sectors, both lemmas
  • bq_gravity_rotor.py · the gravity rotor: dRG, flat quadratic, Galilean coframe, gμν, known metrics, Einstein tensor
  • bq_core.py · basis, four-vectors, operator order, channel rules

Only Python 3 and SymPy are needed. A four-gradient acts on everything to its right; when it stands last, the product is evaluated on a test field ψ.

Conventions used throughout

All coordinates are real; the imaginary unit sits in the basis element T̂ = i1̂, so the Lorentz signature enters through the basis and not through index lowering. The space-time basis T̂, Î, Ĵ, K̂ and the spin-norm basis 1̂, σ̂I, σ̂J, σ̂K together span the full biquaternion algebra; a product of two space-time vectors always needs both.

The superscript T is the BQ time-reversal operation, AT = −a0T̂ + a·K̂: not the matrix transpose, not conjugation and not multiplication by T̂. In every product the left factor stands to the left, so a four-gradient on the left acts on what follows it.

Each model computes its results from the explicit 2×2 matrices and shows a numerical check of the stated rule; the Python sources repeat every product as an exact symbolic check. The BQ definitions in the papers remain authoritative; pages and scripts are a reproducibility aid.
