BQ Lorentz Covariance of the Triple Product

A triple product E = D(ATB) multiplies a four-vector, boosted as U−1DU−1, with a bilinear, boosted as UCU−1. The inner U−1U cancels, so E is boosted like a four-vector again: EL = U−1EU−1. Its minquat sector eTT̂ + eK·K̂ and its pauliquat sector e11̂ + eσ·σ̂ each transform as a four-vector, so equations such as ∂B = μ0J and JB = F keep their form in every inertial frame. How the three kinds of product transform

  • Vector A = a0T̂ + a·K̂: AL = U−1AU−1, and its adjoint (AL)T = UATU.
  • Bilinear C = ATB: CL = (UATU)(U−1BU−1) = UCU−1, a similarity; the norm is invariant and the K̂ and σ̂ channels mix.
  • Triple E = DC: EL = (U−1DU−1)(UCU−1) = U−1EU−1, vector-like again. Every odd product of four-vectors and adjoints is vector-like, every even product is a similarity, as for the grades of a Clifford algebra.
  • Two four-vectors in one. U−11̂U−1 = coshψ 1̂ − sinhψ σ̂I mirrors U−1T̂U−1 = coshψ T̂ − sinhψ Î, so (e1, eσ) transforms exactly like (eT, eK). Three invariants follow: eT² − eK², e1² − eσ² and eTe1 − eK·eσ.

What covariance means here

If DC = X holds in one frame, then DLCL = U−1(DC)U−1 = U−1XU−1 = XL holds in the boosted frame: the equation keeps its form, and X must itself be a four-vector-like object. A sector that vanishes in one frame vanishes in all, so the homogeneous Maxwell equations (the pauliquat sector of ∂B) and the BQ Bianchi identities hold in every frame.
