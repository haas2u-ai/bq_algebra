BQ Field Quadratics

A bilinear field such as the electromagnetic field B = ∂TA has the form F = f 1̂ + k·K̂ + s·σ̂. Its time adjoint flips the σ̂ part, FT = f 1̂ + k·K̂ − s·σ̂. Two quadratic products follow: the field square FF, which holds the Lorentz invariants, and FTF, which holds an energy density and its flux in a chosen frame. Choose a field from the BQ papers.

Choose the field

The general rule for a product FG of two such fields, with G = g 1̂ + m·K̂ + t·σ̂, follows from the multiplication table:

FG = (fg − k·m + s·t) 1̂ + (k·t + s·m) T̂
+ (fm + gk + k×m − s×t)·K̂ + (ft + gs + k×t + s×m)·σ̂

For FF take G = F; for FTF take the first factor with s → −s.

Invariant and observer-dependent

  • FF is covariant. A field transforms as F → UFU−1, so FF → UFFU−1. Its 1̂ and T̂ channels commute with U and are Lorentz invariant. For a pure bivector field (f = 0) the square has only these two channels.
  • FTF is observer-dependent. The time adjoint transforms as FT → U−1FTU, so FTF → U−1FTU²FU−1: not a similarity transformation. Energy density and flux belong to a chosen time direction, and the product carries that dependence.
  • Moving to the space-time sector. T̂ commutes with every basis element. Multiplying by T̂ sends 1̂ → T̂, T̂ → −1̂, K̂ → −σ̂ and σ̂ → K̂, so T̂FTF places an energy–flux pair in the minquat channels T̂ and K̂.

Sources and status

The electromagnetic products BB and BTB, their invariants and the energy–Poynting reading follow de Haas (2026), BQ electromagnetism and fluid dynamics, eq. 92–106 and 133; the fluid products HH and HTH follow eq. 277–280 of the same paper. The angular-momentum bivector, the interaction bilinear and the fluid bilinear are included because they have the same 1 + 3 + 3 form; their quadratics are computed with the same rule, and their readings are marked as extensions.
