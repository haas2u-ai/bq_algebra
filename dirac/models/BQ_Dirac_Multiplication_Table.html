BQ-Dirac Multiplication Table

At the Dirac level the BQ algebra lives in 4×4 complex matrices, in the Weyl representation. Every one of its sixteen basis elements is a 2×2 block matrix whose blocks are BQ-Pauli elements: the Dirac level is the Pauli level one storey up. A product of two Dirac elements is therefore a block product whose four blocks are BQ-Pauli products, and it can be checked a second time as an ordinary 4×4 complex matrix product.

β0 = [[0, T̂], [T̂, 0]],  βi = [[0, K̂i], [−K̂i, 0]],  αi = β0βi,  ΣI = βJβK,  χ = −β0βIβJβK,  β̃μ = χβμ

All 256 products land on exactly one basis element, with sign ±1 and no factor i. Pick a cell to see both factors in their Pauli block content, the block product term by term, and the 4×4 product.

The sixteen basis matrices and their Pauli content

Grades: grade 0 grade 1, vector grade 2, boost bivector grade 2, spin bivector grade 3, pseudovector grade 4, pseudoscalar. Blocks use the BQ-Pauli basis 1̂, T̂ = i1̂, Î, Ĵ, K̂, σ̂I, σ̂J, σ̂K, with K̂i = T̂σ̂i. 𝟙 is the 4×4 unit.

Notes

  • Definitions. Only β0, βI, βJ, βK are entered by hand, from their Pauli blocks. The other twelve elements are computed on this page as block products: αi = β0βi, ΣI = βJβK (cyclic), χ = −β0βIβJβK, β̃μ = χβμ.
  • Off-diagonal vectors. The vector βK is block off-diagonal, [[0, K̂], [−K̂, 0]]. The block-diagonal matrix diag(K̂, −K̂) is not one of the sixteen basis elements; diagonal blocks belong to the even grades 0, 2 and 4.
  • Even and odd. Even grades (𝟙, α, Σ, χ) are block diagonal, odd grades (β, β̃) block off-diagonal: a product of two odd or two even elements is even, a mixed product is odd.
  • Check. Each product is computed twice, by blocks through the BQ-Pauli table and as a 4×4 matrix product, and compared entry by entry; the result is matched against all sixteen ± basis matrices.
