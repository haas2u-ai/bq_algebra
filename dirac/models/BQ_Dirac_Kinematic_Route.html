BQ-Dirac Kinematic Route to Isorapidity

The second branch of the rotor hierarchy differentiates the four-velocity itself. At the Dirac level the local rotor boosts the rest frame by one similarity, Ug = Qg(cβ0)Qg−1 with Qg = diag(G, (G†)−1), and its material derivative along the flow is one commutator with the Dirac Maurer–Cartan form:

Ag = DUg/Dτ = [Ωτ, Ug],  {Ug, Ag} = D(Ug²)/Dτ = 0,  a0 = c DΓ/Dτ

The orthogonality of velocity and acceleration is the derivative of the Clifford square Ug² = −c²𝟙, exact in the rapidities. So isorapidity, DΓ/Dτ = 0, is zero power along the flow, and the spatial acceleration of an isorapid flow is purely transverse: the river turns but does not speed up.
